Getting started

How do I add my API keys?

Open Account Center, then Settings and keys. Add a Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter key and press Save and Apply. Keys stay encrypted on your device and are only sent to the provider you choose.

Is Glint free?

Glint is free. You bring your own provider keys. Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter offer free models, and you can add paid keys for GPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI or Mistral.

Can I install Glint as an app?

Yes. Glint is a progressive web app. Use Add to Home Screen on your phone or the install icon in your desktop browser. It also works offline for chats you already have.

Using Glint

What is Townhall?

Townhall is a round table where several AI models debate your question and a chair writes the final answer. Open it from the sidebar or at /townhall.

How does Auto-pick choose a model?

Glint detects whether your message is about code, research or writing and switches to the strongest model among the providers you added keys for. Pick a model yourself and Auto-pick steps aside. You can turn it off in the plus menu.

What does extended thinking do?

It asks the model to reason more carefully before it answers. Replies can be slower but are often more accurate on hard problems.

Can I create images, video and music?

Yes. Use the mode chip in the chat bar to switch between Chat, Image, Video and Music. These use Gemini models and need a Gemini key.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Ctrl or Cmd plus K searches chats. Enter sends, Shift plus Enter adds a new line, and Esc closes dialogs.

Privacy and data

Who can see my chats and keys?

Nobody but you. Chats and keys are encrypted in your browser before they sync, and our server only stores ciphertext. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

What is a temporary chat?

A chat that is never saved or synced. It disappears when you switch away or close the tab.

How do I delete my data?

Open Account Center, then Data and privacy. You can delete chats, or delete your account and every synced item.

How do I import memory from another AI?

Account Center, Personalization, Import. Copy the prompt, paste it into your old assistant, then paste its answer back into Glint.
