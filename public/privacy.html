Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Glint is built so that we hold as little about you as possible. This page explains what is stored, where, and what you can do about it.

What stays on your device

  • Your chats, settings, personalization, background image and provider API keys are stored in your browser and encrypted there with AES-GCM.
  • Your API keys are sent only to the provider you chose (for example Google, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter) when you send a message. They never pass through our server.

What our server stores

If you create an account to sync across devices, our server stores your account identifier, a login verifier, session tokens, and your data as ciphertext. Your master key is wrapped by your password and your recovery code, and we never see either. We cannot read your chats or keys, and we cannot recover them if you lose both your password and recovery code.

Third parties

  • AI providers. The text you send in a message goes to the model provider you select, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Providers may log or train on data according to their own policies.
  • Web search. When web search is on, your query may be sent to services such as DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, Tavily or Jina Reader to fetch pages.
  • Hosting. Glint is hosted on Vercel with an Upstash Redis database, which process standard connection data such as IP addresses for security and rate limiting.
  • Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Cookies and tracking

Glint does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It uses browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) and a service worker cache to keep the app working, remember your settings and work offline. These are essential to the app and stay on your device.

Shared links

If you publish a chat link, a text-only public snapshot is stored so anyone with the link can read it. You can stop sharing at any time from the chat menu, which removes the snapshot.

Your rights and deletion

You can export your data, delete individual chats, or delete your account and all synced ciphertext yourself from Account Center in the app. If you are in the EU, UK, India or another region with data protection rights, you may also ask us to access, correct or erase your data using the contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Glint is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note it in the app.

Questions: contact form or the contact page.
