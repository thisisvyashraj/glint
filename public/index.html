Questions, answered.

How does Auto-pick choose a model?

Glint detects whether your message is about code, research or writing, then picks the strongest model from the providers you added keys for. Your own choice always wins: pick a model by hand and Auto-pick steps aside until the next task type.

How do I contact you or delete my data?

Use the contact page. You can also delete your account and synced data yourself from Account Center in the app. See the Privacy Policy for every detail.

Is Glint free?

Glint itself is free. You bring your own provider keys, and Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter offer free models, and you can add paid keys for GPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI or Mistral.

Who can see my chats and keys?

Your chats and keys are encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your password. Shared links are the exception: they are public, text-only snapshots that you create and can delete any time.

Can I bring my memory from another AI?

Yes. Personalization gives you a prompt to paste into your current assistant, then you paste its reply into Glint.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Glint installs as an app on iOS and Android and works as a full-screen workspace.
